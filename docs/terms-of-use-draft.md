# Polycircle — Terms of Use (Pre-release Draft)

**Policy version:** `2026-08-alpha-v1`  
**Status:** Pre-release working draft — requires qualified legal review before public distribution.

These Terms are being used to exercise the product's mandatory acceptance flow during development and closed testing. They are not a substitute for final counsel-reviewed Terms, Privacy Policy, app-store disclosures, or jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## 1. Adults only

Polycircle is an adult dating, relationship, and community product. You must be at least 18 years old to create or use a member account. You must provide truthful age information and complete any age-assurance step required by the app store, device platform, or applicable law.

## 2. Consent and boundaries

A match, connection, message response, relationship label, or prior relationship never creates consent to sexual messages, intimate media, in-person meetings, continued contact, or access to private information. Respect blocks, ended connections, stated boundaries, and withdrawn consent.

## 3. User-generated content

You are responsible for content you create, upload, send, or display. You may not use Polycircle to post or facilitate:

- sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors;
- non-consensual intimate content;
- harassment, stalking, bullying, coercion, or credible threats;
- hate or dehumanizing abuse targeting protected or marginalized groups;
- impersonation, fraud, scams, deceptive solicitation, or malicious spam;
- doxxing or publication of another person's sensitive private information without permission;
- illegal goods, illegal services, or other unlawful conduct;
- malware, attempts to compromise accounts or systems, or abuse of Polycircle safety controls.

Polycircle may use automated safeguards, reports, human review, account restrictions, content removal, suspension, evidence preservation, and other proportionate safety measures. Context matters; automated filtering is not the final moderation decision.

## 4. Reporting and blocking

Members can report concerning users or content and can block other members. Blocking is a safety boundary and does not require the other person's agreement. Reports must not be used to harass, retaliate against, or knowingly make false allegations about another person.

## 5. Relationship information

Relationship cards and Circle information describe the account owner's representation unless Polycircle explicitly indicates that another person has verified or consented to that representation. Do not use relationship features to impersonate, expose, or endanger another person.

## 6. Safety and emergencies

Polycircle is not an emergency service, law-enforcement service, medical provider, therapist, or crisis-response provider. Product safety tools can reduce app-level interaction but cannot guarantee personal safety outside the service.

## 7. Moderation and enforcement

Polycircle may investigate reports and take proportionate action, including warnings, content restrictions/removal, feature limitations, temporary suspension, permanent account action, or preservation of relevant evidence where required for safety, appeals, legal obligations, or incident response. Final public Terms must define notice, appeals, retention, and applicable dispute processes with legal review.

## 8. Privacy

Polycircle intentionally limits some sensitive exposure, but the final public release requires a separate Privacy Policy explaining data collection, use, retention, deletion, processors, rights, and contact information. Acceptance of these Terms must not be treated as acceptance of a Privacy Policy where separate consent or notice is required.

## 9. Account termination

Members can request account deletion through the product. Polycircle may also restrict or terminate accounts that violate these Terms, the Community Guidelines, safety requirements, or applicable law. Data deletion and retention behavior remains subject to the documented retention/deletion policy and final legal review.

## 10. Pre-release limitation

During alpha/beta testing, features may change, fail, or be withdrawn. Testers should not rely on Polycircle as the sole repository for important information. No public distribution should occur using this draft as the final legal Terms.
