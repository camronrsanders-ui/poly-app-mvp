## 2. Adults only

Polycircle is intended only for people age 18 or older who are legally able to use a dating/community service in their jurisdiction. Accounts for minors are prohibited.

## 3. Consent and member conduct

A match, connection, prior relationship, or prior exchange never establishes consent to sexual messages, intimate media, meeting in person, continued contact, or any particular relationship structure.

Members must respect boundaries and revocation of consent. Harassment, stalking, threats, hate-based abuse, coercion, non-consensual intimate content, scams, impersonation, and deliberate privacy violations are prohibited.

The final public Terms should incorporate or link the reviewed Community Guidelines.

## 4. Relationship descriptions

Circle/relationship cards are user-generated descriptions. Polycircle does not verify a relationship merely because one member names or describes another person. Members are responsible for respecting others’ privacy when entering names or identifying details.

A future linked/verified relationship feature must use mutual confirmation and requires a Terms/privacy review before launch.

## 5. Accounts and security

Members are responsible for safeguarding account credentials and for activity performed through their account to the extent permitted by law. Members should report suspected unauthorized access through the published support/security channel.

Polycircle may apply security controls including App Check/device integrity signals, rate limits, authentication checks, fraud/abuse controls, and account restrictions.

## 6. Profiles and content

Members retain whatever rights they legally hold in content they submit. The final Terms require a narrowly scoped license sufficient for Polycircle to store, process, moderate, display, and transmit content as needed to operate the service.

That license must not be broader than necessary and must be reviewed before publication.

Members must have the rights and permissions necessary to submit content and must not upload content that violates another person’s privacy, intellectual-property rights, or applicable law.

## 7. Profile photos and protected media

Profile photos may be processed for security, compatibility, metadata removal, moderation, and protected delivery.

Private Vault is currently disabled and should not appear in public Terms as an available feature. Before any private-media feature is enabled, the Terms must specifically address consent, rights to share, moderation, removal, evidence preservation, recipient conduct, and the fact that the product cannot guarantee prevention of screenshots or copying outside Polycircle.

## 8. Messaging

Messaging is intended for active connections. Blocking or ending a connection may terminate access to the conversation under current product rules.

Members must not use messages for harassment, threats, scams, non-consensual sexual content, or other prohibited conduct.

Final message-retention language must match the approved data-retention policy.

## 9. Safety reports and moderation

Polycircle may review reports and take proportionate action such as warnings, feature restrictions, suspensions, bans, content removal, evidence preservation, or referrals where appropriate and lawful.

Moderation decisions should follow the internal moderation runbook and documented appeal/escalation procedures. Public Terms must not promise response times or outcomes the operation cannot reliably meet.

## 10. Blocking and ending connections

Members may block another member or end a connection. These actions can close current interaction and revoke applicable private access. Unblocking does not automatically restore a prior match, conversation, or permission.

Members may not evade another person’s block or create alternate accounts to continue unwanted contact.

## 11. Account availability

Polycircle may restrict or suspend an account for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or violations of the Terms/Guidelines, subject to applicable law.

The public Terms should distinguish voluntary profile visibility controls from moderation/security suspension.

## 12. Account deletion

Members may request account deletion through the product. Deletion is a trusted cleanup workflow and may require recent authentication. A failed cleanup can place the account into a paused recovery state until deletion can be completed safely.

Some information may be retained only where required or justified for legal/safety reasons and according to the final published retention policy. This section must be synchronized with the Privacy Policy and data-retention matrix before publication.

## 13. Service changes and availability

Features may change, be suspended, or be discontinued. Security-sensitive or experimental features may remain disabled until release gates pass.

The Terms should avoid guaranteeing uninterrupted service or that every member will receive matches/connections.

## 14. Fees

Polycircle currently has no finalized paid-product terms in this repository. Do not publish subscription, renewal, refund, virtual-item, or payment language until the business model and payment provider are actually defined.

## 15. Third-party services

The service uses infrastructure providers such as Firebase/Google Cloud. Future third-party integrations should be reviewed for privacy/security and reflected in applicable disclosures.

## 16. Disclaimers and liability

Appropriate service disclaimers, warranty language, liability limitations, statutory carve-outs, and consumer-law protections must be drafted/reviewed for launch jurisdictions. Do not copy generic internet boilerplate into production Terms.

Polycircle is not an emergency service and cannot guarantee the identity, intent, truthfulness, safety, or conduct of another member.
