# Polycircle Business & Product Record

This folder is the business-facing record of Polycircle's product, progress, decisions, and future readiness.

## Start here

### [Current Business Profile](current-business-profile.md)
A concise snapshot of what Polycircle is, what has been verified, current readiness, important differentiation, and the immediate next milestone.

### [Product Journey & Milestones](product-journey-and-milestones.md)
The chronological story of how Polycircle was built: major milestones, difficult problems, product and engineering decisions, lessons learned, verification evidence, and why each milestone mattered.

This record is intentionally preserved for future investors, developers, partners, contributors, and potential strategic buyers so they can understand **how the product evolved**, not only see the finished result.

### [Investor & Acquisition Readiness Plan](investor-readiness-plan.md)
The long-term plan for market research, customer discovery, monetization, financial modeling, fundraising, diligence, partnerships, and acquisition readiness.

### [Approved Product Experience Visuals](approved-product-experience-visuals.md)
Approved product-experience direction and design references. Roadmap visuals must remain clearly distinguished from already released functionality.

## Documentation rule going forward

Every material product milestone should be added to the journey record when it is verified. Strong entries include:

- date / development period;
- starting problem or goal;
- what changed;
- how it was verified;
- important failure or surprise, when useful;
- lesson learned;
- business significance;
- source-control / test / design evidence;
- completion status.

The goal is to preserve Polycircle's institutional memory from early development through launch, growth, fundraising, and any future strategic transaction.
