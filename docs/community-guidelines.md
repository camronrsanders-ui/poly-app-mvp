# Polycircle Community Guidelines — Draft

Status: product/safety draft for review before public distribution.

Polycircle is for adults building consensual connections across polyamory, ethical non-monogamy, open relationships, relationship anarchy, solo polyamory, friendship, community, and other relationship structures. Members do not need to fit one relationship model, identity, or label to be treated with respect.

## 1. Adults only

Polycircle is for people age 18 and older.

Do not create an account if you are under 18. Do not use Polycircle to seek sexual or romantic contact with a minor, share sexual content involving a minor, sexualize a person you know or suspect is a minor, or misrepresent a minor as an adult.

Content that appears to involve child sexual abuse or exploitation is prohibited. Polycircle may restrict access and follow applicable legal/reporting requirements when such material is identified.

## 2. Consent is required

Consent applies to communication, dating, relationships, sexual activity, photographs, private media, and sharing information about other people.

A match is not consent to sex, sexual messages, intimate media, or continued contact. A past yes is not a permanent yes. Consent can be withdrawn.

Do not pressure someone to share intimate photos, move to another platform, meet in person, disclose private information, change their relationship boundaries, or continue a conversation after they have clearly declined.

## 3. No unsolicited intimate media

Do not send or expose another member to unsolicited nude, sexual, or otherwise intimate imagery.

Private Vault, if and when enabled, is designed around explicit request/accept and per-recipient access. A technical ability to share something never overrides the other person's consent. Blocking, ending a connection, withdrawing a request, or revoking a share ends access through Polycircle.

Do not redistribute, repost, record, threaten to distribute, or publish another person's intimate content without their permission.

## 4. Respect relationship structures and boundaries

Polyamory and ethical non-monogamy require communication and consent among the people involved. Members may describe their own relationships and boundaries, but they may not falsely claim another person has agreed to a relationship, arrangement, or public identification.

A name or relationship card entered by one member is that member's description. Unless Polycircle later provides an explicit mutual-verification feature, it does not mean the named person has confirmed the relationship or consented to being represented.

Do not shame, harass, or target someone because they are monogamous, polyamorous, non-monogamous, partnered, single, hierarchical, non-hierarchical, solo poly, relationship-anarchist, or use another consensual relationship structure.

## 5. Respect identity

Do not attack or demean people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, HIV/health status, body type, or other protected/personal characteristics.

Use the names and pronouns people provide. Deliberate, repeated misgendering or identity-based harassment may result in enforcement.

Members may self-describe their gender, orientation, pronouns, and relationship identity. Do not demand that another member prove an identity simply because it differs from your expectations.

## 6. No harassment, threats, or stalking

Do not repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop. Do not threaten violence, sexual violence, outing, blackmail, employment consequences, immigration consequences, or disclosure of private/intimate information.

Do not use alternate accounts to evade a block, suspension, or ban. Do not track, stalk, or attempt to locate another member beyond information they intentionally shared with you.

## 7. Protect private information

Do not publish another person's home address, workplace details, phone number, private email, financial information, immigration information, health information, private legal information, or other sensitive identifying information without permission.

Do not ask for passwords, verification codes, authentication tokens, banking credentials, or access to another person's account.

Polycircle profiles should use only the amount of location information a member is comfortable sharing. Exact location should not be treated as required for participation.

## 8. Be authentic; do not scam or impersonate

Do not impersonate another person, create deceptive profiles intended to defraud members, solicit money through romance scams, manipulate members into financial transactions, or falsely present yourself as Polycircle staff/moderation.

Commercial spam, mass unsolicited promotion, phishing, credential theft, and automated scraping are prohibited.

## 9. Photos and media

Only upload media you have the right to use and share. Do not upload another person's intimate content without their consent.

Profile images may be processed, resized, stripped of metadata, and reviewed before they become available through protected delivery. Approval of a photo does not verify the person's identity or endorse the profile.

If intimate-media features are enabled later, additional consent, retention, moderation, and sharing rules will apply.

## 10. Safety tools are available without permission from the other person

Members may block, report, or end a connection. You do not need another member's approval to use these safety tools.

Unblocking someone does not automatically restore a prior match, chat, or private-media share. Ending a connection is intended to close the connection and associated access rather than create a temporary hide state.

## 11. Enforcement

Depending on severity, pattern, evidence, and risk, Polycircle may take actions such as no action, warning, content removal, feature restriction, temporary suspension, or account ban.

Severe safety/privacy reports may be prioritized over ordinary reports. Polycircle should limit moderator access to sensitive evidence and retain it only according to the approved safety/privacy retention process.

Enforcement should be based on behavior and policy, not disagreement with consensual adult relationship structures or identities.

## 12. Reporting emergencies

Polycircle's report and block tools are product safety tools; they are not emergency services. Members facing immediate danger should use the emergency resources appropriate to their location and circumstances.

## Before these guidelines become final

Before external beta, this draft should be aligned with the final Privacy Policy, Terms, data-retention rules, moderation runbook, app-store requirements, and any applicable legal review. Private Vault must remain disabled until its separate safety/release gate is completed.
