# Polycircle — Post-Launch Product Vision

> This document is intentionally an **after-plan**. It must not distract from the current priority of getting the core Polycircle application stable, runnable, secure, and tested across supported platforms.

## Product essence

**Polycircle shouldn't dictate how people must love. It should give people understandable language, safer tools, trustworthy resources, and thoughtful product mechanics that help them love, grow, and connect with greater communication, consent, and care.**

**Discover helps you meet. Circle helps you understand how you're connected. Resources help you learn and grow. Reconnection acknowledges that relationships can change without pretending the history never happened.**

These statements should be preserved as product and business-positioning principles. Future branding, pitch materials, product strategy, partnership discussions, and feature prioritization can refine the wording without losing the underlying idea.

## Post-launch product pillars

### Meet — Discover
Help people find compatible connections while respecting reciprocal preferences, privacy, consent, safety controls, and relationship context.

### Understand — Circle
Develop Circle into Polycircle's signature interactive relationship map: a fluid, privacy-aware representation of partners, relationship structures, and connected circles rather than a conventional flat list.

### Grow — Community Learning & Support
Provide trustworthy educational language, relationship tools, communication resources, and carefully maintained safety/help information. Polycircle should support people who are learning as well as people already experienced with polyamory and ENM.

### Evolve — Past Connections & Reconnection
Recognize that relationships can end, change, pause, and sometimes reconnect. Build explicit mutual-reconnection mechanics without silently resurfacing former connections and without weakening permanent-end or block boundaries.

## Business perspective

Polycircle's differentiation should not depend solely on matching mechanics. The longer-term value proposition is a relationship platform built around the lifecycle of human connection: discovering people, understanding relationship networks, learning healthier relationship skills, navigating change, and accessing community/safety resources.

This gives future business work several possible directions without committing the product prematurely: community partnerships, educational collaborations, relationship-health resources, events/community spaces, premium convenience features, and other services that remain consistent with the core mission.

Business decisions should not compromise safety, privacy, consent, or the usefulness of core help resources. Safety-critical information should not be placed behind a premium subscription.

## Priority rule

Until the current application foundation is stable and the agreed test/release gates are satisfied, this document is planning material only. Engineering priority remains:

1. keep the current app runnable;
2. eliminate preventable build/runtime failures;
3. complete Android and iOS validation;
4. preserve Firebase/App Check/security protections;
5. complete the required release-readiness work;
6. only then allocate focused product/design cycles to the post-launch vision.

Future ideas may be captured here or in the development roadmap when they arise, but they should not displace stability, security, testing, or release work unless they fix a current blocker.
